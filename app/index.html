You did the work.
The money comes in 90 days.

Small businesses deliver first and wait 30, 60, sometimes 90 days to get paid. Rent, salaries and suppliers don't wait.

Selling the invoice for cash, called factoring, fixes that. Today it means paperwork, slow approvals, and trusting that the invoice isn't fake or already sold to someone else.

Why on-chain

The rules are code.
Not promises.

01

Confirmed by the client

Only the client's own wallet can confirm a debt. No confirmation, no sale.

02

No double sale

Every invoice has exactly one holder. Only that holder can sell it, and never to two buyers at once.

03

Proof that holds up

The client's confirmation, the amount and the due date are public and can't be edited. If it doesn't pay, that record is evidence for a claim.

04

Public payer record

Every payment is recorded as on time or late, in public. Investors see a client's record before they buy.
